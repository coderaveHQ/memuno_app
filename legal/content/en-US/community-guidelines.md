# Community Guidelines

## Goal
Memuno should stay fun, creative, and safe.

## Not allowed
- Harassment, hate speech, threats, or targeted abuse.
- Sexual exploitation or content involving minors.
- Illegal content, non-consensual intimate imagery, or doxxing.
- Spam, scams, impersonation, or fraud.

## Content expectations
Share only content you have rights to use and avoid posting personal data of others without consent.

## Reporting and blocking
If you see harmful behavior, use in-app report and block controls. Reports are reviewed by the moderation process.

## Enforcement
Violations may lead to content removal, temporary restrictions, or account termination.
