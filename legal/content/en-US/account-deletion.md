# Account Deletion

## In-app deletion
You can delete your account in the app under Settings -> Delete account.

## Web deletion request
If you cannot access the app, open this request page and verify ownership via email:
{{deletion_request_url}}

## What happens after deletion
- Your account access is revoked.
- Profile and relationship data is removed or anonymized.
- Some storage references may be cleaned asynchronously.

## Verification
External deletion requests are processed only after email verification and explicit confirmation on the hosted deletion page.
